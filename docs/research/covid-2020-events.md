## Game-day mapping rule (explicit)

"Game day" = the trading day whose screen first shows the event.

- Before open or during trading on a trading day: game day = that same day (player sees it on that day's close screen).
- After 16:00 close, weekend, or market holiday (Good Friday 2020-04-10): game day = the NEXT trading day.
- If the exact time could not be verified, the event is mapped to the later (safe) day and flagged "time?" in the Timing column.

Confidence: A = URL loads and the phrase was seen. B = phrase seen via search extract, but the URL blocks scripted access (HTTP 403/406) or timing is inferred.
